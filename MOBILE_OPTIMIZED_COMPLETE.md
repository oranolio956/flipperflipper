- Stats grid: 2 columns
- Tables: Horizontal scroll

- Hamburger menu appears

- Smaller text sizes
- Compact padding
- Bottom navigation visible
- Sidebar: 260px wide
- Hide less important columns

### **Landscape Mode**
- Optimized sidebar width (240px)
- Compact padding
- Better use of horizontal space

## 🚀 **How It Works on Mobile**

### **Opening the Dashboard:**
1. Tap hamburger menu (top-left)
2. Sidebar slides in from left
3. Background dims with overlay
4. Tap anywhere outside to close

### **Navigation:**
- **Bottom bar**: Quick access to Home, Targets, Commands, Files, More
- **Sidebar**: Full navigation when needed
- **Swipe right**: Open sidebar from left edge
- **Swipe left**: Close sidebar

### **Touch Targets:**
- All buttons: Minimum 44x44px
- Nav items: Full-width, easy to tap
- Icons: Large and clear
- Spacing: Generous for fat fingers
